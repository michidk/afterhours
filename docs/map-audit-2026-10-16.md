# Generated map audit, 16 October 2026

Seed 713 was reviewed with close and elevated views of all nine landmarks, plus three building sites flagged by a terrain-height scan. The review saved 42 matching before-and-after screenshots. Screenshots were taken headless at 1280 × 900. The shared browser timed out.

The evidence viewer loads the game's baked vertex buffers and complete chunks before taking each frame. It uses fixed lighting and solid buildings. It omits dynamic entities, destructible props, shadows, sign text textures and animated sea water. These images establish static placement, not gameplay, water appearance or graphics performance.

## Fixes

- Airports, parks and signs were baked without terrain heights. Both runways were underground. Every baking group now receives the loaded terrain, and the fixture baker also receives the full city. Legacy parking and service surfaces now add ground height to their surface and markings.
- Building placement checked only the centre after the final terrain changes. Hills could bury walls and windows at the footprint edge. Placement now checks corners, terrain-edge intersections and interior terrain vertices. It raises each base above the highest ground and omits sites exceeding the existing foundation's depth. Seed 713 keeps 410 buildings, compared with 413 before the fix.
- Free and fly cameras faded buildings around their inspection target. Fading now applies only in player-follow mode, and switching modes restores visibility.

The regenerated artifacts were prepared separately during verification. The existing managed game preview was then restarted to regenerate and load the corrected city. Original generated files were backed up locally, and unrelated source edits were preserved.

The regenerated terrain, road graph and landmark positions match the original artifact. A [building comparison](screenshots/map-audit-2026-10-16/building-comparison.json) records the omitted sites. A [surface check](screenshots/map-audit-2026-10-16/surface-check.json) confirms that all 70,216 saved asphalt and sidewalk vertices clear the terrain when the renderer's height offsets are applied.
